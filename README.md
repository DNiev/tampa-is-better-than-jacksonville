# tampa-is-better-than-jacksonville

A fifteen-sheet rebuttal to a thirteen-slide deck.

On 4 September 2026 a friend of ours sent Naty and Diego a very good slide deck
arguing that they should leave Miami for Jacksonville. It was well designed,
carefully sourced, and honest enough to concede the places where Miami wins.

It also compared exactly two cities.

We lived in Jacksonville. We left. This is the third column.

**Read it:** https://dniev.github.io/tampa-is-better-than-jacksonville/
**PDF:** [`Tampa_Rebuttal.pdf`](Tampa_Rebuttal.pdf)

### On the numbers

Every figure is sourced on sheet A-14, and where the original deck's numbers are
used they are kept with its citations so both documents can be held open at once.
The central argument uses the original deck's own cost-of-living source, on its
own update date, with one city added.

Sheet A-12 lists five things Jacksonville genuinely does better. That sheet is
not a courtesy. A rebuttal that concedes nothing persuades nobody.
